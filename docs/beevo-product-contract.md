# Beevo Product & Commercial Contract (Source of Truth)

All copy, pricing tables, calculators, FAQs, legal disclosures, and structured data across the Beevo website MUST conform strictly to this confirmed contract.

## 1. Product Definition & Honest Methodology
- **Product:** Beevo — an AI-powered illustrated cartoon storytelling and explainer video SaaS.
- **Core Output:** Professionally composed animated storytelling MP4 videos constructed from AI-written narration scripts, dynamically planned storyboards, consistent AI-generated cartoon illustrations, natural AI narration with timestamp alignment, smooth camera motion, transitions, captions, and licensed background audio.
- **Explicit Boundary:** Beevo is **not** marketed as a continuous frame-by-frame raw AI video generator. Transparency about how scenes, illustrations, motion, and voiceover work together is a core trust pillar.

## 2. Production Technology Pipeline
1. **OpenAI:** Intelligent storytelling, scriptwriting, narrative pacing, and dynamic storyboard planning.
2. **fal.ai:** Original cartoon illustration generation and reference-image editing for consistent characters across scenes.
3. **ElevenLabs:** Natural AI voice generation and precise narration timestamp alignment.
4. **Beevo Dynamic Timeline Engine:** Synchronizes visual scenes, transitions, and pacing directly to real narration timestamps.
5. **FFmpeg:** Applies smooth camera movement (pan/zoom motion graphics), transitions, synchronized captions, and final audio/video composition into a downloadable MP4.
6. **Cloudflare R2:** Private, secure asset and final video storage.

## 4. Confirmed Credit & Usage Rules
- **Metering Rate:** `100 credits = 1 minute` (`25 credits = 15 seconds`).
- **Multi-Project Flexibility:** Credits can be split freely across multiple projects of different lengths.
- **Single-Project Duration Cap:** Up to **10 minutes** per individual video project.
- **Payment Type:** Strictly **one-time credit purchases**—never a recurring monthly or annual subscription.
- **Credit Expiry Policy:** Purchased credits do not expire under Beevo's published credit policy.
- **Failed Generation Protection:** If a video generation fails due to a system or pipeline error, credits for that failed generation are not permanently lost and are automatically restored/refunded back to the user's credit balance.
- **Dynamic Scene Count:** Beevo does not lock users into a rigid image count per minute; the storyboard engine determines how many illustrations each story genuinely needs based on narration moments and selected pacing.
