# Beevo Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Essence
- **Brand Name:** Beevo (wordmark styled in friendly, confident bold lowercase: `beevo`)
- **Category:** AI-powered illustrated cartoon storytelling and explainer video platform
- **Core Promise:** *"Turn your ideas into animated cartoon videos."*
- **Brand Personality:** Cheerful, inventive, warm, transparent, dependable, and craftsmanlike. Never hyped, dark, sterile, or gimmicky.

## 2. Positioning & Honest Voice
Beevo transforms a user's idea or complete script into a polished animated storytelling video composed of AI-written scripts, dynamic storyboards, consistent cartoon illustrations, natural AI narration with real timing alignment, animated camera motion, transitions, captions, and licensed background music/SFX—exported as a clean downloadable MP4.

### Voice Rules
- **Clear over clever:** Explain what Beevo actually does in plain, welcoming sentences.
- **Technically honest:** Always describe Beevo as composed animated cartoon storytelling built from AI illustrations, narration, and motion graphics—never claim continuous raw generative video.
- **Fact-first:** Never publish invented user counts, star ratings, fake testimonials, made-up customer logos, crossed-out fake discounts, or artificial urgency timers.
- **Label demos clearly:** All sample stories (`The Life Cycle of a Bee`, `Save Our Planet`, `The Solar System`, `Healthy Habits for Kids`) are presented transparently as *Illustrative Beevo demos*.

## 3. Dual Visual Identity: Icon Mark vs. 3D Mascot
Beevo strictly separates its **brand logo** from its **mascot illustration system**:

1. **Simple Geometric Icon Logo (Mandatory for UI & Brand Chrome):**
   - Used in the site header, footer, favicon, social metadata badges, and future Studio navigation across all four development phases.
   - Minimal geometric bee silhouette built from bold, crisp vector curves (rounded striped capsule body, symmetrical geometric wings, subtle honeycomb-inspired poise). Zero tiny facial details that blur at `16 px`.
2. **3D Beevo Mascot Character (Editorial & Marketing Artwork Only):**
   - Bright honey-yellow rounded bumblebee with two clean black horizontal stripes, a short dark fuzzy collar, oversized round black-rimmed glasses, expressive warm brown eyes, friendly curved smile, two curved dark-tipped antennae, delicate translucent pale-blue wings, and small charcoal arms/legs.
   - Used intentionally on the homepage hero, step flight-path accent, select illustrative demos, visual style comparisons, and final CTA panel—not stamped repetitively on every subpage card.

## 4. Brand Color Identity
Beevo lives in warm morning daylight:
- **Primary Canvas:** Warm ivory-white (`#FFFEFB`) and pure card white (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Warm Alternate Surface:** Soft butter cream (`#FFF9E9`) and deeper honey cream (`#FFF4D8`)
- **Signature Accent:** Beevo Honey Yellow (`#FFC928`), Hover Gold (`#F2B800`), Text/Detail Honey (`#C88A00` for high-contrast small labels, `#D99A00` for large display emphasis)
- **Ink & Neutrals:** Charcoal Ink (`#171717`), Warm Body (`#36332D`), Muted Stone (`#6E695F`), Hairline Warm Border (`#ECE7DB`)
- **Prohibitions:** No full-width dark marketing sections, no neon purple/blue SaaS gradients, no glassmorphism blur slabs.

## 5. Photography & Illustration Art Direction
- One unified visual family across the homepage: polished, family-film-quality 3D cartoon renders with warm sunlit lighting, shallow depth of field, tactile matte/satin toy materials, and generous negative space.
- Zero baked-in text, letters, logos, or UI buttons inside generated raster images. All labels (such as the three wooden signpost boards `Describe it`, `Generate it`, `Download it`, play overlays, and visual-style captions) are rendered as real semantic HTML/SVG elements.
